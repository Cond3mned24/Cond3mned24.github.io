About me

I'm Jeff, I’ve been part of the Resident Evil modding community since 2021, starting out just exploring and downloading mods from forums. By April 2022, I got into actually creating my own, and I’ve been learning and improving ever since.

Over time, I’ve worked on mods for Resident Evil HD, Revelations, RE4 Remake, and Resident Evil 5, with RE5 being my main focus. Most of what I do involves model work, retexturing, and some rigging, especially through commissions I’ve been taking recently.
